We introduce A2D, a training-free framework that transfers and composes autoregressive post-training updates in diffusion language models. Across recent diffusion language models, A2D improves instruction following, mathematical reasoning, and coding without additional training or inference-time computation. Figure 4(a) shows that combining autoregressive and diffusion updates can outperform either update alone.
